# Tokens and on-chain assets

## Tokens and on-chain assets

- **INT** — Yumo Yumo's transferable utility token, issued on Solana as an SPL token (decimals = 6, total supply = 99,000,000,000). *See: 04 Tokenomics Mechanics.*
- **bINT** — Off-chain accounting credit that tracks a user's verified contribution. Eligible balance becomes an INT claim entitlement at a fixed 1:1 ratio in epoch settlement. *See: 04 Tokenomics Mechanics.*
- **Proof-of-expense SBT** — A Token-2022 NonTransferable token minted once per account to a user's wallet, marking the account as a verified expense contributor. *See: 04 Tokenomics Mechanics, 06 Verifiable Publication.*
- **SPL Token** — Solana Program Library token standard. The "ERC-20 of Solana." Used for INT.
- **Token-2022** — Solana's extension-enabled token standard. Used for the proof-of-expense SBT with the NonTransferable extension.
- **Soulbound** — Token behaviour bound to a single wallet for the lifetime of the asset.
