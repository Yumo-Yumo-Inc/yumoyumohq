# Identity Spending Personality Layer

Patterns presents a spending identity the user can recognize and share — built from receipt observations, not from a clinical personality test.

## Product direction

We ship an **identity + radar** model:

- A readable class / archetype name grounded in evidence chips
- A six-axis radar fed by real receipt-derived motors
- Weekly deltas when history is dense enough
- A shareable identity card
- A community / tribe layer that stays empty until real peer data exists

The older pattern-stream card feed is not the primary surface. Observation motors still feed the radar and the “why?” evidence panel.

## Six grounded traits

Current spending-identity traits:

| Trait | What receipts tend to show |
|---|---|
| Impulsive | Off-routine, late, or one-off spikes |
| Hunter | Promo-heavy, deal-seeking baskets |
| Explorer | High merchant / category variety |
| Hedonist | Comfort and treat categories recurring |
| Loyal | High concentration in the same merchants |
| Planner | Stable routines, lower off-pattern variance |

Missing data leaves an axis empty or low-confidence. We do not invent a full personality from three receipts.

## Money scripts (internal lens)

Financial therapy literature describes money scripts such as status, worship, avoidance, and vigilance. We may use these as **internal** interpretation lenses. On the user surface we stay with observable traits and evidence chips. We do not label someone with a therapy script in the UI.

## What research allows — and what it does not

Spending data can support stronger signals for materialism-like and self-control-like patterns than for a full Big Five diagnosis. Our stance:

- Lead with high-signal traits
- Treat weaker inferences as hints or hide them
- Position the page as curiosity + action, never as a psychological verdict

## Evidence chips (“why?”)

Every identity claim links back to concrete observations:

- “12 late-evening baskets in 30 days”
- “64% of café spend at one chain”
- “Promo lines on 9 of your last 15 grocery trips”

If we cannot point to receipts, we do not show the claim.

## Community layer

Tribe membership, identity leaderboards, and “places your tribe likes” are designed into the product but stay empty-state until cohorts are real. No fabricated percentages, no fake nearby lists, no invented peer ranks.

## Share card

The identity card is for recognition and continuity. It shows the class name, radar silhouette, and a short evidence line. It does not expose private receipt detail or anti-abuse internals.
